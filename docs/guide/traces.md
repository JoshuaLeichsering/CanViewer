> [!NOTE]
> Trace replay is not in this version of CanViewer yet. This page describes what is
> planned.

Planned: open a log recorded from a CAN bus (candump or Vector ASC), and replay it
through the [decoder](./decoding.md) as a table of decoded frames over time. The log is
read and decoded in your browser; it never leaves your machine.
